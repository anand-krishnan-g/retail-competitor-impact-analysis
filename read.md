# Retail Competitor Impact Analysis

### Measuring the Impact of Competitor Store Openings on Retail Performance

**Project Type:** End-to-End Retail Analytics Case Study  
**Tools:** SQL | Python | Pandas | Power BI  
**Dataset:** Rossmann Store Sales — Kaggle  
**Status:** In Progress

---

## 1. Business Problem

Retail businesses operate in highly competitive markets. When a new competitor opens near an existing store, it may affect customer footfall, sales performance, and revenue growth.

Understanding the impact of competitor entry is essential for retailers to identify underperforming stores, assess potential revenue losses, and develop strategies to protect market share.

This project investigates the relationship between competitor store openings and retail store performance using historical sales data.

**Key Business Question:**

How does the entry of a nearby competitor affect existing retail stores, and how can businesses quantify the potential impact on sales and customer footfall?

## 2. Project Objectives

- Analyze store sales performance before and after competitor openings.
- Compare sales trends between stores with and without recorded competitor openings.
- Investigate how competitor distance relates to store performance.
- Evaluate changes in customer footfall and average sales per customer.
- Assess the influence of promotions, seasonality, and store characteristics.
- Explore a difference-in-differences approach to estimate the potential impact of competitor entry, subject to data suitability and assumptions.
- Translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.

## 3. Dataset

**Source:** [Rossmann Store Sales — Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/rossmann-store-sales/data)

The Rossmann dataset contains historical daily sales information for 1,115 retail stores, along with store-level information about nearby competitors and promotional activity.

### Key Data Fields

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Store | Unique store identifier |
| Date | Sales transaction date |
| Sales | Daily store sales |
| Customers | Number of customers visiting/purchasing at the store |
| Promo | Whether the store ran a promotion |
| CompetitionDistance | Distance to the nearest competitor |
| CompetitionOpenSinceMonth | Approximate month the competitor opened |
| CompetitionOpenSinceYear | Approximate year the competitor opened |
| StoreType | Store classification |
| Assortment | Store assortment category |

### Dataset Limitations

- Data is aggregated at store-day level rather than individual customer level.
- Individual customer retention, churn, and migration cannot be directly measured.
- Competitor opening information may be missing or approximate.
- Observed sales changes do not automatically establish a causal competitor impact.

The analysis will account for these limitations when interpreting results.

## 4. Analytical Approach

### Step 1 — Data Preparation

- Load and inspect the source datasets.
- Validate store identifiers and join store-level information with daily sales.
- Identify missing values, duplicates, and inconsistent records.
- Construct competitor opening dates where sufficient information is available.
- Prepare analytical tables for SQL and Python.

### Step 2 — Exploratory Data Analysis

Analyze:

- Total sales and average daily sales
- Customer footfall
- Average sales per customer
- Store-level sales trends
- Promotional activity
- Store performance across different locations and categories

### Step 3 — Competitor Impact Assessment

- Identify stores with recorded competitor opening dates.
- Compare sales before and after competitor entry.
- Identify suitable comparison stores.
- Evaluate pre-opening sales trends.
- Investigate relationships between competitor distance and store performance.
- Explore difference-in-differences analysis where the available data supports it.
- Examine potential confounding factors such as promotions and seasonality.

### Step 4 — Performance Driver Analysis

Investigate whether observed sales changes are associated with:

- Changes in customer footfall
- Changes in average sales per customer
- Promotional activity
- Store characteristics
- Competitor proximity
- Seasonal demand patterns

### Step 5 — Dashboard & Business Recommendations

Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard displaying:

- Store performance overview
- Competitor proximity analysis
- Before-and-after sales comparisons
- Customer footfall trends
- Sales performance drivers

Summarize key findings, analytical limitations, and potential business actions.

## 5. Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Business Meaning |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | Total revenue generated by stores |
| Average Daily Sales | Total sales divided by the number of analyzed store-days |
| Customer Footfall | Reported daily customer counts |
| Average Sales per Customer | Total sales divided by total customers |
| Sales Growth (%) | Percentage change in sales between comparable periods |
| Customer Footfall Growth (%) | Percentage change in customer counts |
| Competitor Distance | Distance between a store and its nearest recorded competitor |
| Estimated Competitor Impact | Estimated difference in sales trends between affected and comparison stores, subject to analytical assumptions |
